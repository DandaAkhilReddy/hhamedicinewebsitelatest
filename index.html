About Us

Focused on patients. Not numbers.

HHA Physician Associates is a non-profit organization dedicated to advancing the practice of hospital medicine. We see the practice of Hospital Medicine differently. At HHA, we put caring for patients above all else — because it's not just the right thing to do, it's what medicine was designed to do.

As a uniquely physician-led, physician-founded organization, we advocate for patients during their most vulnerable moments, providing the highest quality, concierge level of care.

“HHA believes it's time to get back to a promise of doing right by the patient first.”

What Sets Us Apart

Committed to Excellence in Hospital Medicine

Physician-Led

Founded and led by physicians who understand the realities of hospital medicine from the inside. Our leadership ensures clinical excellence drives every decision.

Patient-First
