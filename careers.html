Hospitalist Physician

Full-Time Immediate Start Healthcare Plantation, FL

Position Overview

HHA Physician Associates is recruiting a Hospitalist Physician to deliver inpatient care in Plantation, Florida. The role involves managing hospitalized patients throughout their stay, practicing evidence-based medicine within a collaborative setting. HHA is a non-profit organization committed to putting patients first.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver comprehensive medical care including admissions, daily rounds, and discharge planning
  • Diagnose and manage acute and chronic medical conditions for inpatients
  • Coordinate with specialists, nursing staff, and healthcare team members
  • Maintain accurate electronic health records documentation
  • Communicate with patients, families, and caregivers about diagnosis and treatment plans
  • Support quality improvement efforts and clinical guidelines
  • Address urgent and emergent patient needs during shifts
  • Collaborate on discharge planning with case management and social services
  • Apply appropriate diagnostic and procedural codes (CPT, HCPCS, ICD-10)
  • Foster close working relationship with Emergency Department providers

Required Qualifications

  • MD or DO degree from an accredited medical school (or international equivalent)
  • Completion of ACGME-accredited residency in Internal Medicine or Family Medicine
  • Active Florida medical license (or eligible)
  • Active DEA license (or eligible)
  • Board certified or board eligible in Internal Medicine or Family Medicine
  • Strong diagnostic expertise and clinical decision-making skills
  • Electronic health record system experience

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior hospitalist or inpatient medicine background
  • Critical care or geriatrics certifications
  • English/Spanish bilingual capability
  • Quality improvement program experience
